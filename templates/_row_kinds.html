{# ── Row kind: chip + rail ────────────────────────────────────────── Interface Spec §07. Seven kinds sit in one clock-ordered stream and used to look nearly identical. Three independent channels now carry "what kind of thing is this", so no single one has to survive alone: 1. THE CHIP — two capitals in a fixed 26px box. Completely free of colour, and identical on screen, in the PDF and in the sheet. 2. THE RAIL — 4px on the left that differs by PATTERN before it differs by hue: solid, dashed, double, dotted, hatched. This is the one that matters. Cyan greys to 156 and gold to 167 — ten levels apart, invisible on the mono laser these get printed on constantly. A dash against a dot is unmistakable at any grey. 3. THE SILHOUETTE — a crew call is a framed plate, a break is a tinted band, an anchor is a rule. Readable from six feet. Colour is the fourth channel and the only optional one. Nothing here depends on it, which is also what makes it work for a colour-blind reader. Extracted verbatim from templates/schedule/day.html — the day page had the only copy and the OSS templates could not reach it, so the master schedule and the department tabs were speaking a different language about the same rows. The macro bodies below are unchanged; this is a move, not a rewrite. brand.KIND_CODE / KIND_FILL / KIND_LEGEND stay the source of truth for the two exporters, so the codes cannot drift apart.
